## Overview

Wireways are enclosed, rectangular raceway systems designed to house and route multiple electrical conductors in commercial and industrial facilities. Unlike conduit or cable trays, wireways feature removable covers that provide quick, tool-assisted access to internal wiring for installation, inspection, troubleshooting, and future modifications. They are commonly specified for control wiring, lighting circuits, data/communication cables, and low-voltage power distribution where frequent access, high conductor counts, or future expandability are required.

![](/images/wireway-example.png)

Standard wireway sections are manufactured in modular lengths (typically 10 feet) and a range of cross-sectional dimensions (e.g., 4"×4", 6"×6", 12"×12", up to 24"×24"), allowing flexible routing around equipment, through ceilings, or along walls. Covers vary by application:

- Screw Covers: General indoor use with standard protection and aesthetics
- Ventilated Covers: Slotted or perforated where heat dissipation is required
- Hinged Covers: Ideal for control rooms or panels requiring frequent access
- Toolless/Snap-On Covers: Quick-release design, reduces maintenance downtime

## Naming Convention

```
SIZE COVER (MATERIAL) (FINISH) Wireway
```

| Descriptor   | Explanation                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `SIZE`       | Width and depth in inches, such as 4"x4", 8"x8", etc.                                                  |
| `COVER`      | Cover configuration (Screw-Cover, Ventilated-Cover, Hinged-Cover, etc).                                |
| `(MATERIAL)` | Base and cover material of the wireway, common examples include Aluminum, Steel, Stainless-Steel, etc. |
| `(FINISH)`   | Any finishing options that the wireway may come in such as Galvanized, Powder-Coated-Red, etc.         |

### Example Names

- `12"x12" Hinged-Cover Aluminum Wireway`
- `4"x4" Screw-Cover Stainless-Steel Wireway`
- `6"x12" Ventilated-Cover Steel Wireway`

## Typical Units of Measure

| Unit                         | Typical Use                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Foot/Linear Foot (FT/LF)** | Used at the takeoff/estimating stage to calculate total run length before converting to the number of standard sections needed.                                                                                                                                           |
| **Each (EA)**                | Standard unit for straight sections, fittings (elbows, tees, crosses), and accessories (hangers, barriers, closure plates) — wireway is priced and ordered per discrete section or fitting, not by the linear foot.                                                       |
| **Section (EA)**             | The standard way manufacturers describe a straight length — commonly stocked in **12", 24", 36", 48", 60", and 120"** lengths depending on manufacturer and series.                                                                                                       |
| **Kit/Set**                  | Applies to hardware bundled with sections or fittings — most straight sections ship with one gasket and one set of joining screws included; multi-way fittings (tees, crosses) ship with proportionally more (e.g., a cross fitting includes three gasket/hardware sets). |

### Ordering Help

- Confirm whether **gaskets and joining hardware are included** with each section or fitting — this is standard for most manufacturers' straight sections, but multi-way fittings (tees, crosses) typically ship with additional hardware sets, and telescope/adjustable fittings often ship with none.
- Specify the **NEMA type/rating** (1, 12, 3R, 4, 4X) needed for the installation environment before ordering — this determines gasketing, material, and finish, and is not interchangeable after the fact.
- When a run requires a **transition between two wireway sizes**, order the correct reducer bushing (center-hole or edge-hole) as a separate line item — these are sized by the specific "from" and "to" dimensions (e.g., 6" to 4") and are not universal across size combinations.
