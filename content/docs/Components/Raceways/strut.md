## Overview

Strut provides modular, tool-free assembly. Installers can easily mount electrical conduits, junction boxes, cable trays, and lighting systems using spring nuts and bolts without any cutting or welding on-site. This flexibility reduces labor time and allows for rapid changes or reconfiguration when needed.

### Common Materials

- **Mild Steel**: The most common and cost-effective choice, suitable for indoor or dry environments.
- **Stainless Steel (304/316)**: Ideal for corrosive or sanitary environments like chemical plants, food processing facilities, and coastal areas.
- **Aluminum**: Lightweight, corrosion-resistant, and easy to handle, commonly used in solar and marine applications.

### Surface Finishes

- **Pre-Galvanized (Electro-Galvanized)**: Zinc-coated before forming, suitable for indoor use.
- **Hot-Dip Galvanized (HDG)**: Dipped in molten zinc after fabrication, providing superior corrosion resistance for outdoor or industrial applications.
- **Powder Coated**: Offers added protection and color customization for aesthetic or identification purposes.
- **Plain (Uncoated)**: Used for welding or where finish is not a concern.

### Sizes & Dimensions

- **1-5/8″ x 1-5/8″ (41 mm x 41 mm)**: Also known as the standard or “**deep**” channel. Offers high load capacity and is used in most structural support applications.
- **1-5/8″ x 13/16″ (41 mm x 21 mm)**: A shallower profile ideal for lighter-duty support, such as conduit runs and cable trays. Known as the standard "**shallow**" channel.
- **Double or Back-to-Back Channels**: Two struts welded or fastened together for extra strength—used in long spans or heavy-load scenarios.

### Slot and Hole Types

- **Solid (No Holes)**: Provides maximum strength, typically used when drilling is not needed.
- **Half-Slot (Slotted One Side)**: Allows partial adjustability; common in wall-mounted installations.
- **Full-Slot (Longitudinal Slots Along Entire Channel)**: Offers maximum flexibility for mounting and alignment.

## Naming Conventions

```
(DEPTH) HOLES (MATERIAL) (FINISH) (MEASUREMENTS) Strut
```

| Descriptor       | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `DEPTH`          | Refers to the standard depth (and therefore measurements) that can be used. Options include (Shallow, Deep). If not present, then the measurements option should be included with a depth and width of the strut.        |
| `HOLES`          | Refers to the hole type present on the strut, whether there are no holes present (Solid), with holes along the strut (Slotted), or elongated holes (Elongated-Slotted).                                                  |
| `(MATERIAL)`     | Defines the optional material specification where typical materials include Steel, Aluminum, Stainless-Steel, etc. If left off, assume the strut should be the standard steel material.                                  |
| `(FINISH)`       | If specified, a specific finish can be applied such as Pre-Galvanized, HDG, Powder-Coated (COLOR). If left off, assume a plain finish.                                                                                   |
| `(MEASUREMENTS)` | Defines the measurements of the custom strut if the depth parameter is left off. There should exist two measurements here: the width and depth of the strut in normal measurement form such as 1-1/4"x1-1/2" as example. |

### Example Names

- `Deep Slotted Strut`
- `Shallow Elongated-Slotted Aluminum Strut`
- `Solid Stainless-Steel HDG 1/2"x1-1/2" Strut`
- `Deep Solid Strut`

## Units of Measure - Linear Feet

All strut is measured in the trade length of feet. Depending on order size, suppliers may utilize factored increments in their quoting. Strut is typically cut to meet exact measurement requirements by the job site.

## Ordering Help

- **Specialty metals/finishes**: Stainless steel, aluminum, fireproof, or heavy-gauge channels often carry 1–4 week lead times.
- **Accessories** are the #1 cause of job-site delays; always bundle or confirm separate POs or check vendor portal for "kitted" strut solutions if available; reduces line-item count, minimizes errors, and often cuts lead times.
