## Overview

A base, in this context, refers to a **strut post base** (also called a strut channel post base, base plate, or Unistrut base post) — the fitting that anchors the bottom of a vertical strut channel post to a floor, slab, or other flat surface, forming the foundation of a freestanding support structure. Strut post bases are the vertical counterpart to the general-purpose brackets documented on the Brackets page: where a bracket typically connects strut to a wall or joins strut sections together, a base specifically transfers the load of an upright post down into the floor or foundation.

Strut post bases are used to build freestanding racks, equipment stands, pipe and conduit support columns, solar equipment mounting frames, and shelving systems using standard strut channel — most commonly in mechanical rooms, rooftop equipment areas, and industrial floor-mounted raceway/pipe support runs.

![](/images/base-example.png)

Strut post bases are selected based on:

1. **Mounting orientation:**
    - **Center-mount base** — the strut channel sits centered on the base plate, typically used when the post stands away from any wall or obstruction.
    - **Edge/flush-mount base** — the strut channel mounts flush to one edge of the base plate, used when the post needs to sit close to a wall while still anchoring to the floor.
2. **Hole count and pattern:**
    - **Narrow 2-hole base** — a compact base with two mounting holes, suited to lighter-duty or space-constrained installations.
    - **4-hole or 8-hole square base** — a larger base plate (commonly 4"×6" or 6"×6") with additional mounting holes for greater anchoring strength and stability, used for heavier-duty or taller post installations.
3. **Post sleeve height** — bases are available with a low-profile welded sleeve or a taller welded sleeve (commonly around 3-1/2") that the strut channel post inserts into and is secured with channel-specific hardware; sleeve height affects how much of the post is captured and stabilized at the base connection.
4. **Channel width and configuration compatibility** — bases are manufactured to match a specific strut channel width (most commonly **1-5/8"**) and may be built for a **single-channel post** or a **double-channel (back-to-back) post**, the latter used for taller or more heavily loaded freestanding columns.
5. **Material and finish:**
    - **Galvanized/electro-galvanized (EG) steel** — the standard finish for general indoor and light outdoor use.
    - **Stainless steel (304/316)** — used in corrosive, washdown, coastal, or outdoor rooftop environments where a standard finish would degrade.
    - **Aluminum** — used where weight reduction or additional corrosion resistance is a priority over the highest load capacity.

**Installation notes worth flagging:**

- Anchor hole size on the base plate (commonly 3/4" on larger 6"×6" plates) must be matched to the actual anchor bolt being used for the specific floor/slab condition (e.g., concrete wedge anchor sizing) — the base plate hole size does not itself specify or supply the anchor.
- For taller or more heavily loaded freestanding posts, confirm whether the application calls for a single-channel or double-channel post configuration, since a single-channel post base is not simply a smaller version of a double-channel base — they are different products with different load capacities.
- Nuts, bolts, and channel nuts needed to secure the strut post into the base sleeve are typically sold separately from the base itself, consistent with other strut-system accessories documented on the Brackets page.

## Naming Convention

When identifying strut post bases for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
(CHANNEL) (SIZE) HOLES (FINISH) (TYPE-)Base
```

| Descriptor  | Explanation                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `(CHANNEL)` | Optional channel size identifier, use 1/2"x1-1/2" nomenclature; assume default strut sizing for the channel size of 1-5/8"x1-5/8"             |
| `HOLES`     | The number of holes on the base plate for fastening to concrete or other materials, with the most common number of four (one in each corner). |
| `SIZE`      | Base plate sizing of the plate itself; common sizes are square, so 6"x6" is common, but rectangular can also be specified.                    |
| `(FINISH)`  | Optional finish specifier for the base itself (Galvanized, Stainless-Steel, etc.)                                                             |
| `(TYPE)`    | Type of base, most common being Strut, but any other type of base can be specified. Assume default of Strut and combine that with "-Base."    |

### Example Names

- `1/2"x1/2" 4-Hole 6"x6" Strut-Base`
- `4-Hole 6"x6" Strut-Base`
- `1-5/8"x1-5/8" 8-Hole 12"x12" Strut-Base`

## Typical Units of Measure

|Unit|Typical Use|
|---|---|
|**Each (EA)**|Standard unit for pricing and ordering — bases are ordered individually or in small quantities matching the number of freestanding posts designed into the layout.|
|**Pack**|Common packaging for smaller, high-volume base sizes, typically **4 or 6 per pack**.|
|**Box**|Bulk packaging for larger commercial or industrial orders; quantity varies by base size and manufacturer.|
|**Case**|Distributor bulk quantity for large projects; multiple boxes per case.|

### Ordering Help

- Always specify **channel width, mount style, hole count, plate size, and finish** together — plate footprint and hole pattern both affect anchoring capacity and must match the intended floor/slab anchor layout.
- Confirm whether the application requires a **single-channel or double-channel post base** before ordering — these are different products, not interchangeable by simply doubling up single-channel bases.
- Order **anchor bolts sized to the actual floor/slab condition** (e.g., concrete wedge anchors) as a separate line item — the base plate's hole size only defines what will fit through it, not what anchor is appropriate for the substrate.
- Match **finish between the base and the strut channel/post** it will support, especially outdoors or in washdown environments, to avoid galvanic corrosion at the connection.
- For **rooftop, coastal, or washdown environments**, confirm stainless steel or aluminum construction rather than a standard galvanized finish before finalizing an order, since these materially affect both cost and lead time.
