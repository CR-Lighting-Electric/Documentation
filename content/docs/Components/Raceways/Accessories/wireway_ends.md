## Overview

A wireway end cap is the flat closure plate used to seal the open end of a metal or nonmetallic wireway run, functionally very similar to the Gutter Ends already covered under Enclosures > Enclosure Accessories — but the two are cataloged separately because wireway and auxiliary gutter are different products under different NEC articles, even though they look nearly identical on the shelf.

![](/images/wireway-end-example.png)

- **Wireway** (NEC Article 376 for metal, 378 for nonmetallic) is a **general-purpose raceway** — a sheet-metal or nonmetallic trough with a removable/hinged cover, used for extended routing of conductors across a room, between equipment, or along a wall, the same basic role as conduit but with accessible cover-off pulling, splicing, and tapping along its length. It has no NEC-mandated length limit and is cataloged under Raceways because that's what it functionally is.
- **Auxiliary gutter** (NEC Article 366, covered on the Gutter Boxes page under Enclosures) is a **supplemental enclosure** mounted directly adjacent to equipment (panelboards, switchboards, meter centers) specifically to relieve termination/wiring congestion at that equipment, and is limited to a 30 ft maximum length precisely because it's meant to stay local to the equipment it supplements rather than serve as a general routing raceway.

Both share the same 20% conductor fill limit (75% at splice/tap points) and similar sheet-metal trough-and-cover construction, which is why their accessories — including end caps/end plates — are so similar in appearance and function. The distinction matters for catalog placement and for confirming the correct product line when ordering, since a wireway end cap and a gutter end are typically not cross-listed as the same part even when dimensionally similar.

Wireway end caps are selected based on:

1. **Size fit** — matched exactly to the wireway's cross-sectional dimensions (e.g., 4"x4", 6"x6", 8"x8"); not a universal fit.
2. **Knockout configuration** — With-Knockouts (for a conduit/cable entry through the closed end) or Blank (plain closure, no entry point).
3. **Material** — matched to the wireway's own material: Steel (standard, bonded as part of the raceway grounding path) or Nonmetallic (matched to nonmetallic wireway runs).
4. **NEMA/Type rating** — matched to the wireway's rating (Type 1 indoor, Type 3R outdoor, higher ratings for washdown/corrosive exposure) so the end cap doesn't become the weak point in the run's overall rating.
5. **Cover-style match** — some wireway lines use a lay-in cover style along their length; the end cap should be ordered from the same product line to ensure a proper seated fit and consistent finish.

## Naming Convention

When identifying wireway end caps for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
SIZE RATING (MATERIAL) (KNOCKOUTS) Wireway-End-Cap
```

### Example Names

- `4"-4" NEMA-1 Wireway-End`
- `6"-6" NEMA-3R Stainless-Steel With-Knockouts Wireway-End`
- `8"-8" NEMA-4 Steel Blank Wireway-End`

### Convention Notes

| Descriptor    | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `SIZE`        | The end cap's dimensions, matched exactly to the wireway's cross-section (e.g., 4x4in, 6x6in, 8x8in).                                                                                             |
| `RATING`      | The NEMA/Type rating (e.g., Type-1, Type-3R, Type-4X, Type-12), matched to the wireway's own rating.                                                                                              |
| `(MATERIAL)`  | Steel (standard, bonded as part of the raceway grounding path), Stainless-Steel (corrosive/washdown environments), or Nonmetallic (matched to nonmetallic wireway per NEC 378). Default to Steel. |
| `(KNOCKOUTS)` | With-Knockouts (for a conduit/cable entry through the closed end) or Blank (plain closure, no entry point). Default to Blank.                                                                     |

## Typical Units of Measure

| Unit | Typical Use |
|---|---|
| **Each (EA)** | Standard unit; wireway end caps are ordered individually per open end in the run. |

### Ordering Help

- Confirm you're ordering from the **wireway product line, not the auxiliary gutter product line** — the two are functionally similar but generally cataloged and stocked separately; a mismatched end cap may not seat correctly even at the same nominal dimensions.
- **Count every open end in the finished wireway layout** before finalizing a quote — the same easy-to-miss line item pattern noted on the Gutter Ends page applies here, since wireway is also ordered and priced by section/length.
- Confirm **size, material, and rating match the wireway exactly**, and match the specific product line's cover style (e.g., lay-in) where applicable.
- Specify **With-Knockouts only where an actual conduit or cable entry is planned** at that end; a blank plate is the simpler, more reliably sealed choice everywhere else.
- Since wireway has **no NEC length limit** (unlike auxiliary gutter's 30 ft cap), longer runs are common — on a long multi-section run, confirm end caps are only needed at the true terminal ends, not between joined sections.
