## Overview

Office supplies covers the consumable and general-use materials needed to run an office environment day to day — paper products, writing instruments, filing and organization supplies, fasteners and adhesives, computers, devices, and general desk accessories. The Office category is organized by brand-specific items rather than by an engineering or code specification, so the naming convention on this page emphasizes **physical size first, then brand**, since those are the two details most likely to cause a wrong-item order if left out.

Common subcategories covered under general office supplies include:

1. **Paper products** — copy/printer paper, notepads, and specialty paper, sized to standard formats:
    - **Letter (8.5" × 11")** — the default size for most U.S. office documents and the standard tray size on most printers/copiers.
    - **Legal (8.5" × 14")** — used for contracts and documents requiring more vertical space; requires a printer tray that extends to accommodate the longer sheet.
    - **Tabloid/Ledger (11" × 17")** — used for larger layouts, spreadsheets, proofs, or presentation materials.
    - **A4 (210mm × 297mm)** — the international/ISO standard size used outside North America; close to but not interchangeable with Letter, since the two sizes are not the same dimensions and can cause scaling or margin issues if substituted.
2. **Writing instruments** — pens, pencils, markers, and highlighters, distinguished by tip type/size, ink or lead grade, and color.
3. **Filing and organization** — file folders, binders, hanging folders, and labels, distinguished by size, cut type, and capacity.
4. **Fasteners and adhesives** — staples, paper clips, binder clips, tape, and glue, distinguished by size and capacity/holding strength.
5. **Computers** - iPads, laptops, towers, etc.
6. **General desk accessories** — staplers, tape dispensers, scissors, and similar reusable tools, distinguished by size/capacity and brand rather than a consumable spec.

**A note on ordering practice:** because paper size names (Letter, Legal, A4, Tabloid) are frequently confused in casual purchase requests, always confirm the specific physical dimensions alongside the size name on any order — a request for "long paper" or "legal paper" should always be clarified to the exact size before ordering, since informal terminology is one of the most common sources of wrong-item office supply orders.

## Naming Convention

When identifying office supplies for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
(SIZE) BRAND (SPECS) ITEM (COLOR)
```

| Descriptor | Explanation                                                                                                                            |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `(SIZE)`   | Optional size descriptor to provide dimensions, or a one-size description; use sizing in the 1-1/2"x3" format for multiple dimensions. |
| `BRAND`    | Specify the brand of the product or supply.                                                                                            |
| `(SPECS)`  | Optional descriptor for specifications of the product; replace spaces with hyphens.                                                    |
| `ITEM`     | The actual item name or product.                                                                                                       |
| `(COLOR)`  | Optional color specifier of the product, if left off assume no color.                                                                  |

_Note_ - If the brand, specs or item name contains multiple words or there are space separations, replace the spaces with hyphens.

### Example Names

- `11" Apple Ipad-Pro Silver`
- `11"x8.5" Staples Copier-Paper`
- `0.7mm Pilot-G2 Black Pen`
- `Logitech MX-Keys-S Black`
- `Apple Pencil-Pro`
- `2M Apple 240W Charging-Cable`
- `32" ViewSonic 1440P Monitor`

## Typical Units of Measure

|Unit|Typical Use|
|---|---|
|**Each (EA)**|Standard unit for reusable desk accessories (staplers, scissors, tape dispensers) and large-format single items.|
|**Pack/Box**|Most common packaging for consumables (pens, pencils, folders, staples), typically **10 to 100 units per pack/box** depending on the item.|
|**Ream**|Standard unit for paper — **500 sheets per ream**, the near-universal packaging basis for copy and printer paper.|
|**Case/Carton**|Bulk packaging for high-volume consumables, commonly made up of multiple reams, boxes, or packs; the standard ordering unit for larger offices restocking centrally.|

### Ordering Help

- Always specify **size and brand together** before any other descriptor — these two fields are the leading identifiers in this category's naming convention specifically because they're the most common source of a wrong-item order (e.g., Legal ordered when Letter was needed, or a generic substitute delivered when a specific brand was required for compatibility with existing stock or equipment).
- Confirm **exact paper dimensions**, not just the size name, on any paper order — Letter and A4 are close enough in dimension to be confused in casual requests but are not interchangeable, and using the wrong one can cause printer scaling, margin, or tray-feed issues.
- Confirm **acceptable substitution policy** with the vendor before ordering — clarify whether a different brand or a close but non-identical size/spec is acceptable if the exact item ordered is out of stock, since this can otherwise cause delays or unexpected substitutions on recurring orders.
- Coordinate **reorder timing and case-quantity ordering** against actual departmental usage rather than assumptions based on headcount alone, since bulk case pricing often makes larger, less frequent orders more cost-effective than small, frequent ones.
