## Overview

A wallplate (also called a cover plate or switch plate) mounts over an electrical box and its installed device — receptacle, switch, or a blank/unused opening — providing a finished appearance and covering the live wiring and box opening. Wallplates are selected based on gang count, device opening pattern, mounting style, material, and, for outdoor or wet locations, weatherproof rating.

![](/images/wallplate-example.png)

Wallplates are selected based on:

1. **Gang count** — the number of device openings/positions covered by a single plate, from 1-gang (single device) up through 10-gang or more for large multi-device layouts. Gang count must match the physical device box configuration, not just the number of devices installed.
2. **Device opening pattern:**
    - **Toggle** — a narrow rectangular cutout for a standard toggle switch.
    - **Decora/Rocker (GFCI)** — a larger rectangular cutout for Decora-style rocker switches, GFCI receptacles, and similar wide-body devices.
    - **Duplex (outlet)** — an oval or figure-eight cutout matching a standard duplex receptacle.
    - **Blank** — no cutout, used to cover an unused gang position or an empty box.
    - **Combination** — a single plate with two or more different opening types (e.g., one toggle plus one Decora/GFCI opening), used where a box holds mismatched device types.
    - **Specialty** — cutouts for cable/coax, phone/data jacks, or other low-voltage connectors.
3. **Mounting style:**
    - **Device mount (standard, screw-on)** — the plate mounts directly to the device's own mounting screws, visible on the plate face; the traditional, most common style.
    - **Screwless (snap-on)** — a two-piece system where a base plate attaches to the device or box, and a separate decorative cover snaps on over it with no visible screws.
    - **Box mount** — the plate mounts to the electrical box itself rather than to the device, used for blank covers or certain combination configurations.
4. **Size** — **standard size** fits typical residential boxes and trim; **midway size** and **oversized/jumbo** plates provide additional coverage where a box opening or existing wall damage is larger than a standard plate can conceal.
5. **Material:**
    - **Thermoplastic/nylon (or thermoset)** — the most common and economical option, available in a wide color range; "high-abuse" nylon variants offer added impact resistance for commercial or high-traffic areas.
    - **Stainless steel** — available in different grades (e.g., 302, 430) and used for a durable, commercial-grade appearance; also common in food service and healthcare settings.
    - **Aluminum, brass, or other decorative metal** — used primarily for aesthetic/architectural finish matching.
6. **Weatherproof rating** — outdoor or wet-location installations require a **weatherproof (in-use) cover** rather than a standard wallplate — typically a die-cast aluminum, gasketed, self-closing hinged cover that keeps the installed device protected from moisture even while a cord is plugged in and the cover is closed around it.

**A note on ordering practice:** gang count, opening pattern, and mounting style must all be confirmed together — a plate's gang count alone does not guarantee it will fit a specific box or match the devices installed in it, since opening pattern and mounting style vary independently of gang count.

## Naming Convention

When identifying wallplates for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
GANG OPENING MOUNT MATERIAL COLOR Wallplate
```

### Example Names

- `1-Gang Toggle Device-Mount Nylon White Wallplate`
- `1-Gang Decora Screwless Nylon Gray Wallplate`
- `2-Gang Duplex Device-Mount Stainless-Steel Stainless-Steel Wallplate`
- `2-Gang Toggle-Decora-Combo Device-Mount Nylon Ivory Wallplate`
- `1-Gang Blank Box-Mount Nylon White Wallplate`
- `1-Gang Duplex Weatherproof Die-Cast-Aluminum Gray Wallplate`
- `3-Gang Decora Screwless Nylon Black Wallplate`

### Convention Notes

|Descriptor|Explanation|
|---|---|
|GANG|The number of device positions covered (e.g., 1-Gang, 2-Gang, 3-Gang). Leads the name, since it's the first physical compatibility check against the installed box.|
|OPENING|Toggle, Decora, Duplex, Blank, Specialty, or a hyphenated combination (e.g., Toggle-Decora-Combo) for mixed-device plates. Must match the actual device(s) installed, not just the gang count.|
|MOUNT|Device-Mount, Screwless, Box-Mount, or Weatherproof (for gasketed in-use covers, which mount differently from a standard plate).|
|MATERIAL|Nylon, Stainless-Steel, Aluminum, Brass, or Die-Cast-Aluminum (the last typically paired with Weatherproof mount).|
|COLOR|White, Ivory, Gray, Black, Brown, or the metal finish name for metal plates (e.g., Stainless-Steel, Brass).|
|Catalog number|Vendor catalogs (e.g., Leviton, Eaton, Hubbell, Pass & Seymour) also carry their own part numbers; provide both the plain-language name and the catalog number when known, since exact opening dimensions and combination layouts are manufacturer-specific.|

| Unit          | Typical Use                                                                                                         |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Each (EA)** | Standard unit for pricing and ordering — wallplates are counted to match the device count on the plan set.          |
| **Pack**      | Common bulk packaging for standard nylon/plastic plates, typically **10 per pack**.                                 |
| **Box/Case**  | Distributor bulk quantity, made up of multiple packs; used for large commercial or multi-unit residential projects. |

### Ordering Help

- Always specify **gang count, opening pattern, mounting style, material, and color** together — a plate order missing the opening pattern risks arriving with the wrong cutout for the actual devices installed.
- Confirm **combination plate layouts** (e.g., one toggle plus one Decora opening) explicitly rather than assuming a standard single-pattern plate will work in a mixed-device box.
- For **outdoor or wet-location receptacles**, confirm a weatherproof/in-use cover is specified rather than a standard wallplate — this is a code requirement (NEC 406.9), not an optional upgrade, and the two products are not interchangeable.
- For **screwless plates**, confirm both the base plate and the snap-on decorative cover are included or ordered together, since some product lines sell them as separate components.
- For **commercial, healthcare, or high-traffic areas**, consider stainless steel or high-abuse nylon over standard nylon for improved durability and a more consistent commercial appearance.
