## Overview

A weatherproof box is a device box built to house a switch or receptacle in an outdoor or wet location — a different product family from the interior Switch Boxes covered elsewhere in this documentation, since it has to keep out rain and moisture rather than just contain wiring. This page covers the two common weatherproof box families and the cover accessory that finishes them.

![](/images/weatherproof-box-example.png)

- **FS/FD cast device boxes** — a cast metal box (aluminum, malleable/cast iron, or stainless steel) with threaded conduit hub entries, used for a single wall-mounted device (switch or receptacle). **FS** ("shallow") is the standard depth, suited to a straightforward single device with modest conductor count; **FD** ("deep") provides more interior depth for heavier wire fill, pass-through conduit, or a bulkier device such as a GFCI receptacle that doesn't fold neatly into a shallow box. Both are rectangular/square in profile and available in single-gang and multi-gang configurations, with hub count and size matched to the conduit entries needed.
- **Bell boxes (round or rectangular)** — a cast surface-mount box with multiple pre-threaded hub openings sealed with closure plugs; the installer removes only the plug(s) where conduit actually enters, so one part number covers a bottom entry, back entry, side entry, or pass-through without needing a different SKU for each configuration. Bell boxes come in both round and rectangular profiles — round is common for a single device or fixture mounted on siding, brick, or a post, while rectangular follows the same footprint as an FS/FD box.

A weatherproof box alone doesn't make the finished device weatherproof — it also needs a matching weatherproof cover:

- **Standard weatherproof (flip-lid) cover** — spring-hinged lid(s) that seal the outlet when not in use and lift to access the device; adequate for a receptacle that's typically left unplugged/uncovered.
- **In-use (while-in-use) cover** — a raised, gasketed bubble enclosure that stays closed and weathertight around a cord plugged in and left running, required by NEC for outdoor receptacles in damp/wet locations where a cord may be connected while the box is exposed to weather.

Weatherproof boxes are selected based on:

1. **Depth (Shallow vs. Deep)** — Shallow for standard single-device use; Deep wherever wire fill, pass-through conduit, or a bulkier device needs more interior room.
2. **Shape** — Round (bell box, common for a single fixture/device on siding or a post) or Rectangular/Square (FS/FD standard, or rectangular bell box).
3. **Material** — Cast Aluminum (standard, lighter weight, bonds to metal raceway), Cast/Malleable Iron (heavier-duty, available hot-dip galvanized for road-salt/harsh-corrosion resistance), Stainless Steel (highest corrosion resistance), or PVC/Nonmetallic (corrosion-proof, no bonding path, pairs with nonmetallic conduit).
4. **Gang count** — Single or Multi-gang, matched to the device count needed at that location.
5. **Hub configuration** — the number and trade size of threaded conduit hubs (commonly 1/2", 3/4", 1"); bell boxes ship with more entries than needed, plugged, so only the entries actually used are opened.
6. **Cover type** — Standard flip-lid for an outlet typically left uncovered, or In-Use for a cord that stays plugged in while exposed to weather — required by code in that scenario, not just a nicer option.

## Naming Convention

When identifying weatherproof boxes for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
(SIZING) (GANG) SHAPE DEPTH-Depth OUTLETS-HUB-Outlets (MATERIAL) Weatherproof-Box
```

### Example Names

- `4" Round Deep-Depth 5-3/4"-Outlets Stainless-Steel Weatherproof-Box`
- `2-Gang Rectangular Shallow-Depth 3-3/4"-Outlets Weatherproof-Box`
- `6" Round 5-1/2"-Outlets Weatherproof-Box`

### Convention Notes

| Descriptor    | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `(SIZING)`    | Optional sizing of the box, which will include two measurements if the shape is Rectangular and the sizing is special, or one if the box is round for the diameter. If the box has a gang count, then omit this sizing and use GANG.                |
| `(GANG)`      | Single or Double (multi-gang), matched to the device count at that location if the box shape is Rectangular.                                                                                                                                        |
| `SHAPE`       | Round (bell box, common for a single fixture/device on siding or a post) or Rectangular (standard FS/FD profile, or rectangular bell box).                                                                                                          |
| `DEPTH`       | Shallow-Depth (standard single-device use) or Deep-Depth (for heavier wire fill, pass-through conduit, or a bulkier device like a GFCI). Default to Shallow-Depth.                                                                                  |
| `OUTLETS-HUB` | Number of hub outlet holes hyphenated with the threaded conduit hub size (e.g., 1/2", 3/4", 1").                                                                                                                                                    |
| `(MATERIAL)`  | Die-Cast-Aluminum (standard, bonds to metal raceway), Malleable-Iron (heavier-duty, available galvanized for harsh corrosion), Stainless-Steel (highest corrosion resistance), or PVC (nonmetallic, no bonding path). Default to Die-Cast-Aluminum. |

### Ordering Help

- Always order the **matching weatherproof cover as a separate line item** — a bare box, even a cast weatherproof one, isn't finished or weathertight on its own; confirm Standard-Flip-Lid vs. In-Use-Cover based on whether a cord will remain plugged in outdoors, which NEC 406.9 treats as a required distinction, not a preference.
- Choose **FD over FS** whenever wire fill is heavier than typical, conduit passes through the box rather than terminating, or the device itself (GFCI, larger dimmer) is bulkier than a standard toggle switch or duplex receptacle.
- For **bell boxes**, order based on the entries actually needed — the box ships with extra threaded openings plugged, so one part number covers bottom-entry, back-entry, side-entry, or pass-through configurations without needing a separate SKU per layout.
- Match **material to the raceway and environment** — cast aluminum is the standard pairing with metal conduit; PVC pairs with nonmetallic conduit and avoids corrosion entirely but carries no bonding path; galvanized malleable iron or stainless steel for the harshest corrosion exposure (coastal, road salt, chemical).
- Confirm **hub size and count** against the actual conduit entries planned — ordering a box with more or fewer hubs than needed either wastes pre-threaded openings or requires field-drilling, which most cast boxes aren't intended for.
