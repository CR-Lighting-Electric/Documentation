## Overview

A mud ring (also called a plaster ring or device ring) is a steel cover mounted to the front of a 4" or 4-11/16" square box — see the Square Boxes page — that does two jobs: it reduces the box's wide opening down to fit a standard device or blank cover, and it sets the correct depth so the device ends up flush with the finished wall surface once drywall, tape, mud, and texture (or plaster) are applied over the rough framing. A square box alone isn't finished with a device or cover directly; the mud ring is what makes that connection, which is why it's treated as a required companion purchase rather than an optional accessory whenever a square box is used for a device location.

![](/images/mud-ring-example.png)

Mud rings are selected based on:

1. **Box size fit** — a mud ring sized for a 4" square box will not fit a 4-11/16" square box and vice versa; the two are not interchangeable, so box size must be confirmed before ordering the ring.
2. **Device configuration:**
    - **Single** — one device opening, for a single switch, receptacle, or similar device.
    - **Double** — two device openings, for two devices side by side.
    - **Single-Centered** — a single device opening centered in what's otherwise a double-gang-sized ring, used to mount one device centered in a wider box (common where bulky wiring behind the device, such as audio/intercom gear, needs the extra depth/width of a larger box without exposing two device openings).
    - **Blank** — no device opening at all, used to close a square box being used purely as a junction point behind a finished wall.
3. **Depth (raised dimension)** — the amount the ring raises the device forward from the box face, commonly available from 1/4" up through 1" in standard increments (1/4", 1/2", 5/8", 3/4", 1"); selected to match the thickness of the wall finish being applied over the framing so the device ends up flush once finished.
4. **Material** — steel is standard for mud rings used with metal square boxes; nonmetallic rings exist for use with nonmetallic box/wiring systems.
5. **Gang count of the finished opening** — independent of the box's own size, a mud ring determines how many devices actually show at the finished wall (e.g., a double mud ring on either a 4" or 4-11/16" box still presents a standard 2-gang opening for the cover plate).

## Naming Convention

When identifying mud rings for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
SIZE (CONSTRUCTION) GANG RAISE MATERIAL Mud-Ring
```

### Example Names

- `4" 1-Gang 5/8"-Raise Steel Mud-Ring`
- `4" 2-Gang 1/2"-Raise Steel Mud-Ring`
- `4-11/16" 1-Gang 3/4"-Raise Steel Mud-Ring`
- `4-11/16" Blank 1/4"-Raise Steel Mud-Ring`
- `4" 1-Gang-Centered 5/8"-Raise Steel Mud-Ring`
- `4-11/16" 2-Gang 1"-Raise Steel Mud-Ring`
- `4" 2-Gang 1/2"-Raise Mud-Ring`
- `4-11/16" 1-Gang Flat Mud-Ring`

### Convention Notes

| Descriptor       | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `SIZE`           | 4in or 4-11/16in — the specific square box size the ring is made to fit; rings are not interchangeable between the two box sizes.                                                                       |
| `(CONSTRUCTION)` | Welded (individually welded seams) or Drawn (single-piece stamped construction, no welded seams); both are standard, code-recognized methods. Default to Drawn.                                         |
| `GANG`           | 1-Gang (one device opening), 2-Gang (two device openings), 1-Gang-Centered (one device opening centered in a double-gang-sized ring), or Blank (no device opening, for a pure junction cover).          |
| `RAISE`          | The ring's raised dimension (e.g., 1/4in, 1/2in, 5/8in, 3/4in, 1in), matched to the finished wall thickness so the device sits flush once the wall finish is complete. Use "Flat" if there is no raise. |
| `MATERIAL`       | Steel (standard, used with metal square boxes) or Nonmetallic (for nonmetallic box/wiring systems). Default to Steel.                                                                                   |

### Ordering Help

- Always confirm **box size (4in vs. 4-11/16in)** before ordering — mud rings are sized specifically to one box, and the wrong ring simply won't seat correctly on the other box size.
- Match **depth to the finished wall thickness** — a common field rule of thumb is sizing the ring about 1/8" deeper than the drywall thickness alone (e.g., 5/8" ring for 1/2" drywall) to account for joint compound, tape, and texture buildup, though this varies by region/company standard, so confirm against local practice or spec.
- Decide **device configuration at rough-in** based on the final device plan — a blank ring for a straight junction box, single or double for standard device counts, or single-centered where one device needs to sit centered in a wider box (common for audio/intercom or other equipment needing extra depth behind it).
- Mud rings are a **required companion purchase** whenever a square box is used for a device location — don't plan to mount a device or cover plate directly to a bare square box.
- Confirm **material consistency** with the box and wiring system — steel rings on steel boxes is the standard pairing; nonmetallic systems should stay nonmetallic throughout for bonding/grounding consistency.
