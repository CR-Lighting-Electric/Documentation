## Overview

A weatherproof cover is the device-plate accessory that finishes a Weatherproof Box for outdoor/wet-location use — see the Weatherproof Boxes page. The box alone isn't weathertight; the cover is what actually seals the opening around the device. There are two functionally different families, and the choice between them isn't a style preference — it's set by whether a cord will be plugged in and left running while the receptacle is exposed to weather.

![](/images/weatherproof-cover-example.png)

- **Standard flip-lid cover** — a spring-hinged lid (or lids, for a duplex opening) that seals the outlet when unused and lifts for access; the receptacle is not weather-protected once something is plugged in, since the lid can't close around a cord.
- **In-use (while-in-use) cover** — a raised, gasketed enclosure (often called a "bubble" cover) deep enough to fully house the receptacle and a plugged-in cord cap, staying closed and weathertight around the cord as it exits through a gasketed opening at the bottom. This is the only cover type that keeps a receptacle protected while something stays plugged in outdoors.

Within in-use covers, **"extra-duty"** is a specific, code-referenced classification (NEC 406.9(B)(1)), not just a marketing term — an extra-duty cover is rated to stay weatherproof in wet locations while a cord remains connected and the cover is closed around it, as opposed to a lighter-duty cover only rated for the closed (unplugged) condition. Extra-duty covers are further split by depth:

- **Standard depth** — adequate clearance for typical plugs and devices.
- **Deep** — additional interior depth (commonly around 3-1/2") for larger plug caps, bulkier devices, or where more clearance is needed behind the closed lid.

Weatherproof covers are selected based on:

1. **Cover type** — Standard-Flip-Lid for a receptacle typically left uncovered/unplugged, or In-Use for anywhere a cord may stay connected while exposed to weather (required by NEC 406.9 in that scenario).
2. **Duty rating** — for in-use covers, Extra-Duty specifically meets the NEC 406.9(B)(1) wet-location-while-in-use requirement; confirm this rating rather than assuming any bubble-style cover qualifies.
3. **Depth** — Standard or Deep, matched to the plug/device size actually being used.
4. **Material** — Die-Cast Aluminum (durable, corrosion-resistant, higher-end) or UV-Rated Polycarbonate (impact-resistant plastic, UV-stabilized, often with a clear lid for visibility of the connected device).
5. **Gang count and device configuration** — Single or Double gang, with device openings sized/configured for duplex receptacles, GFCI receptacles, toggle switches, or round openings (for a photocell, bell, or similar round device) — many products use interchangeable snap-in inserts to cover several device configurations with one base cover.
6. **Orientation** — Vertical or Horizontal, matched to the device's mounting orientation in the box.

## Naming Convention

When identifying weatherproof covers for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
(SIZE) (GANG) TYPE (DUTY) (DEPTH-Depth) (MATERIAL) ORIENTATION Weatherproof-Cover
```

### Example Names


- `1-Gang In-Use Extra-Duty Deep-Depth Dual-Orientation Weatherproof-Cover`
- `2-Gang Flip-Lid Vertical Weatherproof-Cover`
- `1-Gang Blank Die-Cast-Aluminum Weatherproof-Cover`
- `2-Gang Blank Stainless-Steel Weatherproof-Cover`

### Convention Notes

| Descriptor      | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `(SIZE)`        | Optional specifier for special sizing requirements of the cover. If rectangular with gang count, omit and use the GANG specifier.                                                                          |
| `(GANG)`        | Single or Double, matched to the device count at that location.                                                                                                                                            |
| `TYPE`          | Flip-Lid (seals only when unplugged) or In-Use (stays closed and weathertight around a plugged-in cord). Blank is also an option as just a flat blank box cover.                                           |
| `(DUTY)`        | Extra-Duty (meets NEC 406.9(B)(1) for wet locations while in use; applies to In-Use covers) or omit for Standard-Flip-Lid covers, where this rating doesn't apply). Default to Standard-Duty.              |
| `(DEPTH-Depth)` | Standard-Depth or Deep-Depth (additional interior clearance for larger plugs/devices); omit for Standard-Flip-Lid covers where depth isn't a differentiating spec. Default to Standard-Depth for the type. |
| `(ORIENTATION)` | Vertical or Horizontal, matched to the device's mounting orientation. If omitted, defaults to Vertical/Horizontal, meaning both directions work.                                                           |
| `(MATERIAL)`    | Die-Cast-Aluminum (durable, corrosion-resistant) or Polycarbonate (UV-rated impact-resistant plastic). Default to Die-Cast-Aluminum.                                                                       |

### Ordering Help

- Determine **In-Use vs. Standard-Flip-Lid** based on whether anything will ever be plugged in and left running at that outdoor location — if yes, an In-Use, Extra-Duty cover is required by NEC 406.9(B)(1), not just recommended.
- Don't assume every bubble-style cover is **Extra-Duty rated** — confirm the specific product meets NEC 406.9(B)(1) for wet-location-while-in-use protection rather than going by appearance alone.
- Choose **Deep depth** wherever a larger plug cap, a bulkier device (GFCI, some dimmers), or extra clearance behind the closed lid is needed; Standard depth covers most typical duplex receptacle and toggle switch installations.
- Confirm **device configuration/inserts** match the actual device(s) in the box — many in-use covers use interchangeable snap-in inserts covering several configurations (duplex, GFCI, toggle, round openings) from one base cover, so specify what's needed rather than assuming a fixed single-purpose product.
- Match **material to the environment and budget** — die-cast aluminum for a more durable, higher-end finish; UV-rated polycarbonate for a lighter-weight, cost-effective, impact-resistant option that still meets the same code ratings.
