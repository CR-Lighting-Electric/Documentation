## Overview

A square box is a metal junction/device box in a 4" or 4-11/16" square footprint, used wherever more interior volume is needed than a standard single-gang switch box provides — for larger conductor counts, multiple circuits landing in one box, junction/splice points, or as the base box for a single device fed through a plaster ring. Square boxes are a step up in capacity from the Switch Boxes covered elsewhere in this subcategory, and are common in both residential (as a junction point) and commercial/industrial wiring (as the standard device and junction box size).

![](/images/square-box-example.png)

The two standard sizes are:

- **4" square** — the smaller and more common of the two, adequate for most junction and single-device applications.
- **4-11/16" square** ("5-square" on the field) — a larger footprint offering meaningfully more interior volume, used where box-fill requirements exceed what a 4" square can accommodate — heavier conductor counts, multiple circuits, or multi-gang device configurations.

Square boxes come in a few depths — commonly 1-1/2", 2-1/8", and (for 4" boxes specifically) up to 3-1/2" — with deeper boxes providing more box-fill volume. Construction is either **welded** (individually welded seams, generally sturdier) or **drawn** (a single-piece stamped/drawn box with no welded seams); both are standard, code-recognized construction methods, with the choice typically driven by the specific product line and application rather than a strict rule.

A square box is almost never used bare with a switch plate directly on it — because the box itself is wider than a standard device or opening, a **plaster ring** (also called a mud ring or device ring) is mounted to the front of the box to both close down the opening to the size of the actual device(s) and set the correct mounting depth relative to the finished wall surface (drywall or plaster). Plaster rings come in single-device, multi-device, and blank (no device opening, for a straight junction box) configurations, and are ordered as a companion product to the box rather than as part of it — see Convention Notes.

Square boxes are selected based on:

1. **Size** — 4" for standard junction/single-device applications; 4-11/16" where box-fill volume requirements or multi-gang device counts exceed what a 4" box can hold.
2. **Depth** — deeper boxes provide more box-fill volume (NEC 314.16) for higher conductor counts, more devices, or more circuits landing in the same box.
3. **Construction** — Welded or Drawn, both code-recognized; selection is generally driven by product line availability and the manufacturer's spec rather than a functional requirement either way.
4. **Knockout size/pattern** — sized and positioned for the conduit sizes and entry points needed; common knockout sizes include 1/2", 3/4", and 1".
5. **Plaster ring pairing** — the box alone doesn't finish the installation; the correct plaster ring (device opening count/type, or blank for a pure junction box) must be selected to match the final device plan and wall finish thickness.

## Naming Convention

When identifying square boxes for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
SIZE (CONSTRUCTION) (DEPTH-Depth) (KNOCKOUTS-KO) (BRACKET) Square-Box (COLOR)
```

### Example Names

- `4" Welded 1-1/2"-Depth 1/2"-3/4"-KO Square-Box`
- `4" Welded Deep 3/4"-KO Square-Box`
- `4-11/16" Drawn 2-1/8"-Depth 3/4"-KO Square-Box`
- `4-11/16" 3"-Depth 1"-KO Square-Box`
- `4" Welded 3-1/2"-Depth 3/4in Square-Box`
- `4" Deep Special Square-Box`
- `4-11/16" Deep Special F-Bracket Square-Box Red`

### Convention Notes

| Descriptor       | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `SIZE`           | 4-Square (standard footprint with 4" sizing) or 4-11/16in ("5-Square," larger footprint for higher box-fill or multi-gang needs).                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `(CONSTRUCTION)` | Welded (individually welded seams) or Drawn (single-piece stamped construction, no welded seams); both are standard, code-recognized methods. Default to Welded.                                                                                                                                               |
| `(DEPTH)`        | The box's interior depth (e.g., 1.5in, 2.125in, 3in, 3.5in), which along with SIZE determines cubic-inch box-fill volume per NEC 314.16. Default to 2-1/8". Common alias is also "Deep" for this characteristic.                                                                                               |
| `(KNOCKOUTS)`    | The predominant knockout size on the box (e.g., 1/2in, 3/4in, 1in); boxes commonly include multiple knockout sizes, so confirm the full knockout pattern against the specific product when more than one size is needed. Include all knockout sizes. Use "Special" for the typical 1/2"-3/4" Knockout pattern. |
| `(BRACKET)`      | Bracket type affixed to the side of the masonry box for fastening to various locations (F, C, D, S, VMS, etc.) i.e. F-Bracket, C-Bracket, etc.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `(COLOR)`        | Optional color specifier for the box (Red, Blue, etc.)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

- Square boxes are almost always ordered with an accessory ring that goes over the box. Refer to the Rings page for information on those.

### Ordering Help

- Always order the **plaster ring as a separate line item** matched to the box — the box alone doesn't provide a device-mounting surface or set the correct depth to the finished wall; specify device count/type and ring depth based on the wall thickness (drywall vs. plaster, and its thickness) at the point of installation.
- Step up to **4-11/16" ("5-square")** whenever box-fill calculations for the expected conductor/device/circuit count exceed what a 4" box can hold — a common mistake is defaulting to 4" out of habit and hitting a fill violation once conductors are counted.
- Confirm **depth against box-fill needs** before ordering — more/larger conductors, more devices, or multiple circuits landing in one box all call for a deeper box; see the NEC 314.16 reference in Further Resources.
- **Welded vs. drawn construction** is typically not a functional decision — confirm against the specific manufacturer's product line and any project spec requirement, rather than assuming one is inherently required over the other.
- Confirm the box's **knockout pattern** covers all conduit entries needed at that location, including any conduit sizes required for future circuits, since square boxes are frequently used as junction points serving several conduit runs at once.
