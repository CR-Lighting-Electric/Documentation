## Overview

A nut is the internally-threaded fastener that mates with a bolt, screw, or threaded rod to create a clamped joint. Like washers, nuts are a routine companion purchase alongside bolts and threaded rod across nearly every category already covered in this documentation — strut assembly, panel and equipment mounting, grounding hardware, and general fastening — and the right nut is chosen as much by function (structural clamping vs. vibration resistance vs. hand-adjustable vs. rod-joining) as by thread size.

![](/images/nut-example.png)

Nuts fall into a few functional families:

- **Hex nuts** — the standard six-sided general-purpose nut; **Finished Hex** is the common choice for most bolted connections, **Heavy Hex** adds a larger wrenching surface and more thread engagement for higher-load or structural work, and **Jam Nuts** are a thinner, low-profile hex nut used to lock a primary nut in place or to set a fixed position on a threaded rod without adding height.
- **Lock nuts** — nuts built to resist loosening under vibration. **Nylon-Insert-Lock** (also called "nyloc") nuts use a nylon collar that grips the bolt threads with prevailing torque; **Flange-Lock** nuts integrate a serrated washer face for a locking bite plus load distribution in one part; **Flex-Lock** (all-metal) nuts use a slotted, slightly deformed collar for the same prevailing-torque effect without a nylon insert, making them suitable for higher-temperature applications where nylon would degrade.
- **Coupling nuts** — a long, fully-threaded cylindrical nut used to join two lengths of threaded rod end-to-end into a single longer run.
- **Wing nuts** — a nut with two protruding "wings" for hand-tightening and hand-loosening without tools, used wherever a connection needs to be adjusted or removed routinely rather than torqued to a fixed structural spec.
- **Acorn (cap) nuts** — a domed nut that fully covers the end of the bolt or rod, used to protect exposed threads from damage/snagging and to finish an installation cleanly where the fastener end is visible.
- **Flange nuts** — a hex nut with an integrated, non-serrated washer face, spreading load without requiring a separate flat washer; distinct from a flange-lock nut, which adds a serrated locking bite to the same integrated-flange design.
- **Square nuts** — a four-sided nut, most often paired with square-head bolts or used in strut/channel hardware, offering a wider wrenching surface and rotational resistance in slotted applications.

Nuts are selected based on:

1. **Type** — Hex/Heavy-Hex for general and structural clamping, a lock-nut style where vibration resistance is needed, Coupling for joining rod lengths, Wing where hand-adjustment is needed, Acorn where exposed threads must be covered/finished, Flange where an integrated washer face is wanted, and Square for strut/channel or square-bolt applications.
2. **Thread size** — matched to the mating bolt, screw, or threaded rod (e.g., #10-24, 1/4-20, 3/8-16, 1/2-13).
3. **Grade (steel hex/heavy hex nuts specifically)** — Grade 2, Grade 5, or Grade 8, matched to the grade of the mating bolt so the weaker of the pair isn't the limiting factor in the joint; not applicable to stainless, brass, or nylon-insert lock nuts, which aren't graded on this same SAE system.
4. **Material** — Zinc-Plated-Steel (standard, graded per SAE for hex/heavy hex), Stainless-Steel (304/18-8 for general corrosion resistance, 316 for marine/saline/coastal exposure), Brass (nonferrous, galvanic compatibility with brass/bronze hardware), or Nylon (the insert material in nylon-insert lock nuts, not a structural body material on its own).
5. **Application context** — structural and high-load connections favor Heavy-Hex at the correct grade; connections exposed to vibration favor a lock-nut style; rod-joining calls for a coupling nut; anywhere the connection needs routine hand-adjustment calls for a wing nut.

## Naming Convention

When identifying nuts for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
SIZE TYPE (GRADE) (MATERIAL) Nut
```

### Example Names

- `1/4"-20 Hex Grade-5 Zinc-Plated-Steel Nut`
- `3/8"-16 Heavy-Hex Grade-8 Zinc-Plated-Steel Nut`
- `1/2"-13 Hex Stainless-Steel Nut`
- `#10-24 Nylon-Insert-Lock Zinc-Plated-Steel Nut`
- `1/4"-20 Wing Stainless-Steel Nut`
- `3/8"-16 Coupling Zinc-Plated-Steel Nut`
- `1/4"-20 Acorn Stainless-Steel Nut`
- `3/8"-16 Flange Zinc-Plated-Steel Nut`
- `1/4"-20 Jam Stainless-Steel Nut`

### Convention Notes

| Descriptor   | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `SIZE`       | The mating thread size (e.g., `#10-24`, 1/4"-20, 3/8"-16, 1/2"-13).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `TYPE`       | Hex or Heavy-Hex (general/structural clamping), Jam (thin locking/position nut), Nylon-Insert-Lock, Flex-Lock, or Flange-Lock (vibration resistance), Coupling (joining rod lengths), Wing (hand-tightening), Acorn (domed thread cover), Flange (integrated washer face), or Square (strut/square-bolt applications). |
| `(GRADE)`    | Grade-2, Grade-5, or Grade-8 for steel Hex/Heavy-Hex nuts per SAE J995, matched to the mating bolt's grade; NA for stainless, brass, and nylon-insert lock nuts, which aren't rated on this system. Default to no specific grade.                                                                                      |
| `(MATERIAL)` | Zinc-Plated-Steel (standard, graded per SAE), Stainless-Steel (304/18-8 general corrosion resistance, or specify 316 for marine/coastal), Brass (nonferrous/galvanic compatibility), or Nylon (insert material in lock nuts only).                                                                                     |

### Ordering Help

- Match **grade to the mating bolt**, not just to the nut alone — pairing a lower-grade nut with a higher-grade bolt (or vice versa) makes the weaker part the limiting factor in the joint's rated strength.
- Use a **lock-nut style wherever the connection will see vibration** — nylon-insert for standard temperature ranges, flex-lock (all-metal) where heat would degrade a nylon insert, and flange-lock where the integrated washer face is also wanted.
- **Coupling nuts** are the standard way to extend threaded rod runs (e.g., seismic bracing, suspended support) rather than overlapping or welding rod — confirm thread size and rod length needed on both sides of the joint.
- Reserve **wing nuts** for genuinely hand-adjustable connections (access panels, temporary fixturing) — they aren't intended to be torqued to a structural spec and shouldn't be substituted where a torqued hex nut is required.
- Match **material to environment and galvanic compatibility** in the same way as washers and other hardware on this page — zinc-plated steel for standard indoor use, stainless (304 or 316) for corrosion resistance and coastal/marine exposure, and confirm compatibility with dissimilar-metal hardware it will be installed against.
- Like washers, nuts are a **small, high-volume, easy-to-underorder item** — worth keeping standard sizes/types in stock rather than ordering piecemeal per job.
