## Overview

A bolt is an externally-threaded fastener with a formed head, installed through aligned holes in two or more parts and secured with a nut (see the Nuts page) or driven into a tapped hole or wood substrate. Bolts differ from all-thread rod (see the All-Thread Rod page) in having a fixed head on one end rather than being threaded full-length and cut to size, and they're selected as much by head/body style — which determines how the bolt seats, resists rotation, and engages the substrate — as by size, grade, and material.

Bolt types:

- **Hex bolts** — the standard general-purpose bolt with a six-sided head for wrench/socket engagement; the default choice for steel-to-steel connections, equipment mounting, and general structural fastening.
- **Carriage bolts** — a smooth, domed head with a square neck directly beneath it that seats into and resists rotating within a square or round pre-formed hole in wood; standard for wood framing and timber connections where a clean, tamper-resistant finished face is wanted on the head side.
- **Lag bolts (lag screws)** — a heavy-duty, coarse-threaded, tapered-tip fastener with a hex head, driven directly into wood (typically with a pilot hole) rather than paired with a nut; used for heavy structural wood connections such as securing strut, equipment, or framing members to a wood substrate.
- **Structural bolts** — high-strength bolts manufactured to ASTM A325 or A490 specifications, used specifically for structural steel-to-steel connections (e.g., securing unistrut, equipment racks, or support steel to structural framing) where the connection's rated strength must meet a structural engineering specification, not just general hardware-grade fastening.
- **Machine bolts** — a round or hex-head bolt, fully threaded or with a short shank, used in general machinery assembly and equipment mounting where a lower-strength, general-purpose fastener is adequate.
- **Flange bolts** — a hex bolt with an integrated washer face under the head, spreading load without a separate flat washer, similar in concept to a flange nut.
- **Toggle bolts** - a round head bolt with integrated spring threaded toggle that secures bolt around the material, useful in, for example, drywall installations.

Bolts are selected based on:

1. **Type** — Hex for general steel-to-steel and equipment fastening, Carriage for wood connections needing a clean finished head, Lag for heavy-duty direct-to-wood fastening, Structural (A325/A490) for engineered steel connections, Machine for general lower-strength assembly, Flange where an integrated washer face is wanted.
2. **Size** — the diameter-thread designation (e.g., 3/8"-16, 1/2"-13) matched to the mating nut, or a diameter-only callout for lag bolts, which use a tapered wood thread rather than a machine thread pitch.
3. **Grade** — Grade 2 (general-purpose, lowest strength), Grade 5 (medium strength, most common general construction grade), Grade 8 (highest SAE strength, heavy-duty/structural-adjacent use), or A325/A490 for structural bolts specifically, matched to the mating nut's grade and the connection's load requirement; not applicable in the same way to carriage or lag bolts, which aren't SAE-graded.
4. **Material** — Zinc-Plated-Steel (standard indoor), Hot-Dip-Galvanized (exterior/damp locations, standard finish for structural bolts), or Stainless-Steel (304 general corrosion resistance, 316 for marine/coastal).
5. **Length** — measured under the head to the tip, matched to the total material thickness being clamped plus adequate thread engagement/nut protrusion.

## Naming Convention

When identifying bolts for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
SIZE TYPE GRADE MATERIAL LENGTH Bolt
```

### Example Names

- `3/8-16 Hex Grade-5 Zinc-Plated-Steel 2in Bolt`
- `1/2-13 Hex Grade-8 Zinc-Plated-Steel 3in Bolt`
- `3/8-16 Carriage Grade-2 Zinc-Plated-Steel 4in Bolt`
- `1/2in Lag NA Hot-Dip-Galvanized 6in Bolt`
- `3/4-10 Structural A325 Hot-Dip-Galvanized 3in Bolt`
- `3/8-16 Hex NA Stainless-Steel 1.5in Bolt`
- `5/16-18 Flange Grade-5 Zinc-Plated-Steel 1.5in Bolt`

### Convention Notes

| Descriptor     | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SIZE           | The diameter-thread designation (e.g., 3/8"-16, 1/2"-13), matched to the mating nut; for Lag bolts, a diameter-only callout (e.g., 1/2") since lag threads are a tapered wood thread rather than a machine thread pitch. Toggle bolts can also leave off the TPI measurement since they come with a built in toggle fastening measure. |
| TYPE           | Hex (general-purpose), Carriage (wood, clean finished head), Lag (heavy-duty direct-to-wood), Structural (engineered steel-to-steel), Machine (general lower-strength assembly), Flange (integrated washer face), or Toggle (integrated spring toggle).                                                                                |
| LENGTH         | Measured from under the head to the tip (e.g., 1.5in, 2in, 3in, 6in), matched to total clamped material thickness plus adequate thread engagement.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| GRADE          | Grade-2, Grade-5, or Grade-8 per SAE J429 for standard bolts, or A325 / A490 for structural bolts specifically; NA for carriage and lag bolts, which aren't rated on this system.                                                                                                                                                      |
| MATERIAL       | Zinc-Plated-Steel (standard indoor), Hot-Dip-Galvanized (exterior/damp, standard for structural bolts), or Stainless-Steel (304 general corrosion resistance, or specify 316 for marine/coastal). Default to Zinc-Plated-Steel.                                                                                                        |
| Catalog number | Vendor catalogs (e.g., Fastenal, McMaster-Carr, Portland Bolt) also carry their own part numbers; provide both the plain-language name and the catalog number when known.                                                                                                                                                              |

### Ordering Help

- Reserve **Structural (A325/A490) bolts** for connections called out on the project's engineering documents — these aren't a drop-in upgrade over a standard Grade 8 hex bolt; they carry their own installation, tensioning, and inspection requirements specific to structural steel connections.
- Match **grade to the mating nut**, exactly as noted on the Nuts and All-Thread Rod pages — the weaker of the paired components sets the connection's effective rated strength.
- Choose **Carriage over Hex for exposed wood connections** where a smooth finished face is wanted on the head side and the square neck's rotation resistance is useful (no wrench needed to hold the head while tightening the nut).
- Confirm **pilot hole and pre-drilling requirements for Lag bolts** — driving a lag bolt without an appropriately sized pilot hole risks splitting the wood substrate or stripping the thread engagement.
- Measure **length under the head**, not overall bolt length, and confirm it accounts for the full clamped material stack plus enough thread protrusion for the nut and a lock washer if one is used.
- Match **material to environment and galvanic compatibility** with the nuts, washers, and rod it will be installed against, consistent with the rest of this Hardware category.
