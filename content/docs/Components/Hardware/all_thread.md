## Overview

All-thread rod (also called threaded rod, or "all-thread") is continuously-threaded steel or stainless rod stock, sold in standard lengths and cut to size in the field, used wherever a bolted connection needs to run through a long span, anchor into concrete, or be field-adjusted for length rather than using a fixed-length bolt. It's a routine companion purchase alongside the nuts and washers already covered in this category — a run of all-thread is typically ordered with matching hex nuts and flat/lock washers for the actual connection.

![](/images/all-thread-example.png)

Common applications include:

- **Suspended support** — hanging pipe, duct, cable tray, and mechanical/electrical equipment from structural framing above, typically via a beam clamp or insert at the top and a trapeze or direct hanger at the bottom.
- **Seismic bracing** — rigid or cable-restrained bracing runs that tie suspended systems back to structure, sized and rated to resist lateral seismic loads (see the Splices and Zip-Ties pages for related restraint hardware already in this catalog).
- **Concrete anchoring** — set in wet concrete or into a drilled hole with epoxy/mechanical anchor, projecting up to receive equipment, strut, or framing.
- **Strut/channel assembly** — running through strut channel as a threaded stud for shelving, rack, or support assemblies.
- **General through-bolting** — wherever a standard bolt isn't available in the needed length, or the connection needs to be field-cut to an exact dimension on site.

All-thread rod is graded and selected by:

1. **Grade** — the material specification governing tensile/yield strength and intended service:
    - **ASTM A307 Grade A** — the standard general-purpose grade (60 ksi tensile); the default choice for routine hanger and support work with no elevated structural or seismic demand.
    - **ASTM F1554 Grade 36 / 55 / 105** — purpose-built for concrete anchor bolts and structural anchoring, with the grade number indicating minimum yield strength in ksi; Grade 36 is the most common for general anchoring, with 55 and 105 stepping up for higher-load structural anchor applications.
    - **ASTM A193 Grade B7** — a high-strength alloy steel grade for demanding structural, industrial, and high-temperature/pressure service (flanges, pressure vessels, petrochemical work) beyond what standard A307 or F1554 rod is rated for.
    - **SAE Grade 5 / Grade 8** — the same grade system used on hex bolts, occasionally specified for all-thread where the rod is standing in for a graded bolt in a mechanical assembly.
2. **Size (diameter-thread)** — matched to the mating nuts/washers and the load path (e.g., 3/8-16, 1/2-13, 5/8-11).
3. **Material/finish** — Zinc-Plated-Steel (standard indoor corrosion protection), Hot-Dip-Galvanized (heavier protection for exterior/damp locations), Plain-Steel (mill finish, common on B7 rod intended for a separate coating or indoor use), or Stainless-Steel (304 general corrosion resistance, 316 for marine/saline/coastal exposure).
4. **Length** — sold in standard stock lengths (commonly 3 ft, 6 ft, 10 ft, and 12 ft) and field-cut to the exact run needed.
5. **Application/load requirement** — light-duty hanger work stays at A307 Grade A; concrete anchoring calls for F1554; high-load structural, seismic, or high-temperature work steps up to B7 or a higher F1554 grade; corrosive or marine environments call for stainless regardless of the load grade otherwise selected.

## Naming Convention

When identifying all-thread rod for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
SIZE (GRADE) (MATERIAL) All-Thread
```

### Example Names

- `3/8"-16 Zinc-Plated-Steel All-Thread`
- `1/2"-13 F1554-36 Hot-Dip-Galvanized All-Thread
- `5/8"-11 B7 Plain-Steel All-Thread-Rod`
- `1/4"-20 All-Thread`
- `1/2"-13 A307-A Stainless-Steel All-Thread`
- `3/4"-10 F1554-105 Plain-Steel All-Thread`

### Convention Notes

| Descriptor   | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `SIZE`       | The diameter-thread designation (e.g., 1/4"-20, 3/8"-16, 1/2"-13, 5/8"-11, 3/4"-10), matched to the mating nuts and washers.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `(GRADE)`    | A307-A (standard general-purpose), F1554-36 / F1554-55 / F1554-105 (concrete anchor bolt grades, by minimum yield strength in ksi), B7 (high-strength alloy steel, ASTM A193, for demanding structural/industrial/high-temperature service), or SAE-Grade-5 / SAE-Grade-8 (bolt-equivalent grades). Default to A307-A standard. |
| `(MATERIAL)` | Zinc-Plated-Steel (standard indoor), Hot-Dip-Galvanized (heavier exterior/damp-location protection), Plain-Steel (mill finish, common on B7 rod), or Stainless-Steel (304 general corrosion resistance, or specify 316 for marine/coastal). Default to Zinc-Plated-Steel.                                                       |

## Typical Units of Measure

|Unit|Typical Use|
|---|---|
|**Each (EA)**|Standard unit for full stock-length rods (e.g., a single 10ft length).|
|**Foot (FT)**|Used when rod is cut to a specific field length rather than ordered as a full stock length.|
|**Box**|Bulk packaging for smaller-diameter rod in common lengths.|
|**Bundle**|Distributor bulk quantity for larger-diameter or job-lot orders.|

### Ordering Help

- Match **grade to the actual load and application**, not to habit — A307 Grade A covers routine hanger/support work, but concrete anchoring calls for an F1554 grade specifically rated for that use, and high-load structural, seismic, or high-temperature work needs B7 or a higher F1554 grade.
- Confirm **nut and washer grade compatibility** — pairing a high-grade rod with standard-grade nuts (or vice versa) makes the weaker component the limiting factor in the connection, same as with bolted hardware elsewhere in this category.
- For **seismic bracing and life-safety-related suspended systems**, confirm the specified grade and size against the project's engineering documents rather than defaulting to standard-duty A307 rod.
- Order **stock length to minimize field cutting and waste** — for long or repetitive runs (trapeze hangers, bracing), calculate the actual cut lengths needed against available stock lengths (3/6/10/12 ft) before ordering.
- Match **material/finish to the environment** — zinc-plated for standard indoor use, hot-dip galvanized or stainless for exterior, damp, or corrosive locations, and confirm galvanic compatibility with the nuts, washers, and structural hardware the rod will be installed against.
- All-thread rod is typically ordered **alongside matching nuts and washers**, not as a standalone item — confirm quantities of both are included on the same order to avoid a delayed install.
