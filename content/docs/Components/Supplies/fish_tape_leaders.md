## Overview

A fish tape leader (also called a fish rod attachment or tip) is the replaceable working-end piece that threads onto a fish tape or fish rod — see the Fish Tape page — to match the tool to the specific job at hand. Rather than buying a separate fish tape for every situation, the leader/attachment is swapped depending on whether the task is threading through open conduit, steering around obstructions, or hooking and retrieving a conductor that's already in place. Leaders are sold as individual replacement parts or in multi-piece sets, and are a routine consumable/replacement item since the working tip takes the most wear and is the piece most likely to be lost or damaged on a job.

![](/images/fish-tape-leader-example.png)

Common leader/attachment types:

- **Bullet nose** — a rounded, tapered tip that threads easily through conduit with minimal catching; typically includes a line-attachment hole for tying off a pull line or conductor once the leader has been fed through.
- **Hook** — a single-hook attachment used to grab and retrieve a conductor or cable that's already in a crowded space (a wall cavity, a congested conduit run with other cables already present) rather than to thread through open conduit.
- **Whisk** — a multi-strand or splayed attachment that helps guide the rod over obstructions and can roll side-to-side to help steer left or right while fishing, useful in irregular or obstructed cavities.
- **Loop/eyelet** — a simple looped tip (built into some fish tapes directly, or as a separate leader) providing a straightforward tie-off point for pull line or conductor.
- **Illuminated tip** — a lighted leader/tip for visibility in dark cavities or long conduit runs, helping confirm the leader's position or find it from the other end.

Fish tape leaders are selected based on:

1. **Task** — Bullet-Nose for threading through open conduit; Hook for grabbing/retrieving an existing conductor in a crowded space; Whisk for steering around obstructions in an irregular cavity; Loop for a simple tie-off point.
2. **Rod/tape size compatibility** — leaders are sized to fit specific rod diameters (commonly 5/32", 3/16", and 1/4"); not universal across all fish tape/rod products or brands.
3. **Material** — stainless steel is standard for durability and corrosion resistance, especially on hook and bullet attachments that see repeated contact and wear.
4. **Stackability** — some attachments can be threaded onto another attachment (e.g., a hook or bullet threaded onto a whisk) for a combined function in a single pass.
5. **Brand/system compatibility** — leaders are generally brand- and even product-line-specific (e.g., fitting standard fish rods but not a manufacturer's "super rod" series); confirm compatibility with the specific fish tape/rod in use before ordering.

## Naming Convention

When identifying fish tape leaders for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
SIZE TYPE MATERIAL Fish-Tape-Leader
```

### Example Names

- `3/16" Bullet-Nose Stainless-Steel Fish-Tape-Leader`
- `1/4" Hook Steel Fish-Tape-Leader`
- `5/32" Loop Stainless-Steel Fish-Tape-Leader`
- `1/4" Illuminated-Tip Stainless-Steel Fish-Tape-Leader`
- `5/32" Hook Flexible Fish-Tape-Leader`

### Convention Notes

| Descriptor | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `SIZE`     | The rod/tape diameter the leader is made to fit (e.g., 5/32", 3/16", 1/4"); not universal across sizes or brands.                                                                                               |
| `TYPE`     | Bullet-Nose (threading through open conduit), Hook (grabbing/retrieving an existing conductor), Whisk (steering around obstructions), Loop (simple tie-off point), or Illuminated-Tip (lighted for visibility). |
| `MATERIAL` | Stainless-Steel is standard for durability and corrosion resistance. Use "Flexible" for vendor discretion on flexible fish tape leaders.                                                                        |

### Ordering Help

- Confirm **rod/tape size and brand compatibility** before ordering — leaders are sized to specific rod diameters and are often brand- and product-line-specific, so a leader from one manufacturer's standard rod may not fit another's, or a heavier-duty product line within the same brand.
- Keep a stock of the **most-used leader types** (typically Bullet-Nose and Hook) as routine replacement/consumable items — the working tip takes the most wear and is the piece most likely to need replacing or go missing on a job.
- Choose the leader by **task, not habit** — a hook is for retrieving a conductor that's already present in a crowded space, not for threading through open conduit, where a bullet nose is the better fit; using the wrong type slows the pull rather than speeding it up.
- Where **stackable attachments** are supported (e.g., a hook or bullet threaded onto a whisk), consider ordering combinations that let one fishing pass handle both steering and grabbing rather than requiring separate passes with separate leaders.
- For **low-visibility or long dark runs**, an illuminated tip is worth stocking separately to confirm leader position or locate it from the far end of the run.
