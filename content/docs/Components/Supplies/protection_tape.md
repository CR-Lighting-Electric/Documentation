## Overview

Corrosion protection tape is a wrap tape used to seal out moisture and shield exposed metal — rigid/IMC conduit threads and couplings, ground rod connections, bolted splices, and buried or outdoor fittings — from rust and corrosion. It's commonly called "Dottie tape" on the field after L.H. Dottie, one of the most widely stocked brands, the same way "Minnie" has become a generic term for a brand of conduit hanger. The name has genericized to cover the product category regardless of manufacturer.

![](/images/protection-tape-example.png)

Two distinct tape constructions fall under this category:

- **PVC/rubber-resin corrosion tape** (the type most commonly meant by "Dottie tape") — a black PVC film tape coated with a rubber-resin adhesive, wrapped directly over the threaded joint, coupling, or connection with no separate primer needed. This is the everyday choice for wrapping rigid/IMC conduit threads, couplings, and fittings in both above-ground and direct-burial applications, protecting the joint from moisture intrusion and galvanic/atmospheric corrosion.
- **Petrolatum (wax) tape systems** — a petrolatum-saturated fabric or felt tape, used as part of a three-part system: a petrolatum paste primer applied first to prepare the metal surface, the petrolatum tape wrapped over it as the main corrosion barrier, and an outer PVC or utility tape wrapped over that for mechanical and UV protection. This heavier-duty system is the standard for the most corrosion-prone or long-term applications — buried bolted flanges, ground rod and grounding-system connections, valves, and waterworks joint restraints — and is built to standards such as NACE RP0375 and AWWA C217.

Corrosion protection tape is selected based on:

1. **Tape system** — PVC/rubber-resin tape for straightforward direct wrap of conduit threads, couplings, and fittings; petrolatum tape system (primer + tape + outer wrap) for higher-corrosion-risk or long-term buried/grounding applications.
2. **Thickness (mil)** — commonly around 10 mil for PVC corrosion tape, providing puncture and abrasion resistance over the wrapped joint.
3. **Width** — 2" is the standard width for hand-wrapping conduit joints, couplings, and connections; other widths exist for larger fittings or faster coverage.
4. **Application environment** — above-ground exposed connections, direct burial, or submerged/waterworks applications each call for confirming the specific product is listed for that exposure.
5. **Surface preparation** — petrolatum tape systems require a primer coat and clean metal surface for proper adhesion and corrosion resistance; PVC/rubber-resin tape is more forgiving but still benefits from a clean, dry surface before wrapping.

## Naming Convention

When identifying corrosion protection tape for vendor ordering, use the following naming structure, listing attributes in this order:

```
(WIDTH) (THICKNESS) (SYSTEM) (COLOR) Protection-Tape
```

### Example Names

- `2" 8mil Protection-Tape`
- `4" Protection-Tape`
- `Petrolatum 2" 20mil Protection-Tape`
- `Red Protection-Tape`
- `Petrolatum-Outer-Wrap 2" 10mil Protection-Tape`

### Convention Notes

| Descriptor    | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `(WIDTH)`     | The tape's roll width, most commonly 2" for hand-wrapping conduit joints and connections; wider rolls (4") for larger fittings or faster coverage. Default to 2".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `(THICKNESS)` | The tape's gauge in mils (e.g., 10mil for standard PVC corrosion tape, heavier for petrolatum tape). Default to 10mil.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `(SYSTEM)`    | PVC-Rubber-Resin (standard direct-wrap "Dottie tape," no primer needed), Petrolatum (petrolatum-saturated tape, the main barrier layer in a primer/tape/outer-wrap system), Petrolatum-Primer (the paste primer component of that system, sold separately), or Petrolatum-Outer-Wrap (the PVC or utility tape used as the outer mechanical/UV layer over petrolatum tape). Default to PVC-Rubber-Resin. |
| `(COLOR)`     | Black is standard for this category, default to Black.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

### Ordering Help

- For a standard **conduit thread, coupling, or fitting wrap**, order PVC-Rubber-Resin corrosion tape ("Dottie tape") — no primer is needed, and it's the fastest, most common choice for both above-ground and direct-burial conduit connections.
- For **high-corrosion-risk or long-term buried applications** — bolted flanges, ground rod and grounding-system connections, valves, waterworks joint restraints — specify the full petrolatum system (primer, tape, and outer wrap) rather than PVC tape alone, and order all three components together from the same manufacturer to ensure system compatibility.
- **Surface preparation matters**: clean and dry the metal surface before wrapping either tape type, and for petrolatum systems, apply the primer coat first per the manufacturer's instructions — skipping the primer undermines the system's corrosion resistance.
- Confirm **width against the fitting size** being wrapped — 2" is standard for most conduit joints and couplings, but larger fittings or flanges may call for a wider roll to get full coverage without excessive overlap seams.
- The "Dottie tape" nickname is used generically across brands in the field; when matching an existing installation or a spec calling for a specific standard (e.g., NACE RP0375, AWWA C217), confirm the actual product and system rather than ordering by nickname alone.
