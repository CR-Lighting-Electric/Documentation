The Raceway Fittings & Accessories subcategory encompasses all mechanical components used to route, transition, protect, and terminate electrical raceway systems (rigid conduit, EMT, PVC, wireway, cable tray, etc.). These fittings enable directional changes, branch connections, equipment entry points, expansion, and system grounding. Proper selection ensures compliance with NEC bending space and fill requirements, maintains raceway integrity, and supports efficient installation, inspection, and future modifications. Fittings are specified by configuration, trade size, material, environmental rating, and connection method to match project conditions and code mandates.
