Conduit bodies (often abbreviated as "C-bodies") are junction boxes used in wire conduit installations to provide access points for pulling and splicing wires, as well as to facilitate changes in conduit direction. They serve as intermediate connection points between conduit runs and are essential for maintaining code-compliant installations while allowing future maintenance and wire replacement.

![](/images/conduit-body-example.png)

Unlike standard junction or pull boxes, conduit bodies are compact, pre-formed enclosures with knockouts positioned to match common conduit routing configurations. They are installed at bends, offsets, and directional changes where direct conduit bends would make wire pulling impractical or impossible.

## Types of Conduit Bodies

Conduit bodies are classified by their shape and entry configuration, denoted by letter codes that indicate the orientation of the conduit entries relative to the body:

- **LB** – Side entry to bottom exit. Used for 90° bends where conduit enters from the side and exits through the bottom.
- **SB** – Side-to-side entry. Used for straight-through or offset pulls where conduit enters and exits from opposite sides.
- **TB** – Top entry to bottom exit. Used for vertical drops or rises where conduit enters from the top and exits through the bottom.
- **LL** – Large left/right angle. Similar to LB but with a larger body for increased wire bending space.
- **C** – Circular/round body. General-purpose body with multiple knockout options for flexible entry configurations.
- **SC** – Square body. Provides multiple entry points with a square or rectangular shape for complex routing.
- **AC** – Angled corner body. Used for angled bends other than 90°.
- **T** - Tee body that can split a run of wire into two different directions, can look like a "T."

## Materials

Conduit bodies are manufactured in several materials to match the conduit type and environment:

- **PVC** – Used in wet locations and corrosive environments. Available in Schedule-40 and Schedule-80 wall thicknesses, typically used in Schedule-40 for electrical applications.
- **Steel (EMT/IMC/RMC)** – Galvanized steel bodies matching metallic conduit types. Used in general commercial and industrial applications. Inlucdes the **Stainless-Steel** variant as well.
- **Cast-Iron** – Used in hazardous locations and where extra durability is required.
- **Aluminum** – Lightweight option for corrosive environments where steel is unsuitable.

## Naming Convention

When ordering conduit bodies, the naming convention should follow this format:

```
SIZE MATERIAL (SCHEDULE) (CONNECTION) (ADDITIONAL) TYPE Conduit-Body `(COLOR)`
```

| Descriptor     | Explanation                                                                    |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `SIZE`         | The size of the conduit body connection points in inches, for example 1-1/2".  |
| `MATERIAL`     | Material of the conduit body, i.e. Stainless-Steel, Aluminum, Steel, etc.      |
| `(SCHEDULE)`   | Schedule of the PVC if the material is PVC; if left off, assume Schedule-40.   |
| `(CONNECTION)` | The type of connection points the body utilizes, defaults to threaded.         |
| `(ADDITIONAL)` | Any additional specifier; could include a color, brand, etc. Hyphen separated. |
| `TYPE`         | Type of the conduit body designated by the letter codes explained above.       |
| `(COLOR)`      | Any specific color that the conduit body should be ordered in.                 |

### Example Orders

- `1/2" Steel LB Conduit-Body`
- `2-1/4" Stainless-Steel Compression T Conduit-Body`
- `3" Aluminum Screw-Clamp C Conduit-Body Red`
- `1" Steel Threaded LL Conduit-Body Blue`
- `1-3/4" PVC Schedule-80 Push-Connect LB Conduit-Body`
